Overview of "DLS"

Historians have been able to uncover the abbreviated name of the school in which the majority of these events happened. That name is "DLS". Although it is not confirmed to be true, there are some signs that suggest these letters stand for "Debt La Salle". DLS was established in 131 BK, and records of it can be found as late as 1 AK. Some suspect that most of the information is being supressed by certain people and organisations. DLS' current status is currently unknown, but see further information within the sections dedicated to DLS related media.

The Marcellus Saga: Purgatory

Marcellus' Purgatory
Released May 6th 22 BK
Director Steven Stalagmite
Run time 149 minutes

The Marcellus Saga consists of two movies, Marcellus' Purgatory and Marcellus' Final Bout. The first of which, Purgatory, was released in summer of 22 BK to widespread acclaim.

Plot

Having been sealed in a mysterious realm, Marcellus must fight his way through Purgatory in the hopes of escaping as a new man.

The movie begins in the Marcellus household. Marcellus is shown to act as a tyrant over his children, eventually resulting in his eldest son creating Purgatory alongside his brothers, an otherworldy domain created to contain Marcellus.

Marcellus initially entered an area of Purgatory resembling heaven, but upon entering a strange portal he was thrown into a vast hell. As he progressed, he encountered some of his old enemies, and made new ones. It was not long before he discovered his children attempting to destroy the exit to Purgatory. Marcellus was running out of time.

In the end, Marcellus defeated them without breaking a sweat and escaped Purgatory. The movie concludes with Marcellus returning home. In a post credit scene, the youngest son of Marcellus arrives home to find his siblings beaten by Marcellus.

The sequel, 'Marcellus' Final Bout', was released 2 years and 3 days after Purgatory.
